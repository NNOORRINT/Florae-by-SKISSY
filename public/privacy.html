Privacy notice

Plain-language summary of what Planty stores and why.

What we store

  • Your account: your email address, the name you choose, and a securely hashed password (handled by our login provider, Supabase).
  • Your plant checks: the photo you check (resized), the AI's answer and any note you add. These are private to your account.
  • Community posts: posts, photos, replies and likes you share in the community are visible to everyone using Planty, together with your chosen name.

How photos are analysed

When you check a plant, the photo and your note are sent to Anthropic's Claude AI to produce the diagnosis. They are used only to answer your request.

What we don't do

We don't sell your data and we don't show ads.

Your choices

You can delete any saved check from My plants and any post or reply you wrote. To delete your whole account, contact us and we will remove it and everything linked to it.

Contact

Questions or requests: contact the Planty by Skissy team.
